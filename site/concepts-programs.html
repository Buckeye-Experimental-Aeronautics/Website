Programs: layout concepts

Goal: see every active program at a glance, then drop into one program's detail fast. Four patterns, all interactive and built with our real programs. Nothing here is on the live site. Detail text and specs are draft placeholders (marked TBD where we don't have facts yet).

Patterns researched: Hermeus /aircraft (a roadmap list of every aircraft with year and status, then a block per aircraft), Impulse Space's mission table (already on our homepage), and the card and tab patterns common on vehicle pages from launch and defense companies.
