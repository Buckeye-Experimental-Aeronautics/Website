SRR / DCR

System Requirements Review / Design Criteria Review

Are the need, constraints, requirements, success criteria, and initial design problem sufficiently defined?

Authorizes

Begin structured concept development.

Required

New Programs or major scope changes.

Evidence reviewed

  • Need and mission
  • System boundary
  • Requirements
  • Constraints
  • Success criteria
  • Initial concept space
  • Feasibility and risk
  • Initial work structure
  • Plan forward

CoDR

Conceptual Design Review

Has a preferred concept been selected from credible alternatives, with acceptable feasibility and risk?

Authorizes

Adopt the baseline concept and begin preliminary design.

Required

Where meaningful configuration alternatives exist.

Evidence reviewed

  • Requirements and criteria
  • Candidate concepts
  • Trade studies
  • Concept definition
  • Feasibility
  • Interfaces and budgets
  • Risk and uncertainty
  • Work decomposition
  • Plan forward

PDR

Preliminary Design Review

Is the preliminary design complete enough to demonstrate that requirements can be met and interfaces can be controlled?

Authorizes

Begin detailed design and long-lead procurement as authorized.

Required

Every Program.

Evidence reviewed

  • Design baseline
  • Requirements compliance
  • Engineering analysis
  • Interfaces
  • Budgets and margins
  • Risk and safety
  • Verification and validation
  • Manufacturability and implementation
  • Work decomposition
  • Plan forward

CDR

Critical Design Review

Is the detailed design complete, internally consistent, verifiable, and releasable for manufacture or implementation?

Authorizes

Release the design baseline for build or implementation.

Required

Before release of the integrated design baseline.

MRR

Manufacturing Readiness Review

Are the design, materials, tooling, procedures, work orders, personnel, and inspection controls ready to produce conforming hardware?

Authorizes

Begin controlled manufacturing or assembly.

Required

Before integration.

TRR

Test Readiness Review

Are the test article, facility, instrumentation, procedure, hazards, personnel, and data system ready to execute the test safely and usefully?

Authorizes

Begin the approved test activity.

Required

Before an integrated test campaign.

FRR

Flight Readiness Review

Are the integrated vehicle or component, crew, site, procedures, weather limits, contingency plans, and open risks acceptable for flight?

Authorizes

Conduct the authorized flight operation.

Required

Before flight.

Outcomes

ApprovedAll mandatory exit criteria are satisfied.
Conditionally approvedThe work may proceed only within stated constraints while specified actions close.
HeldReadiness is not demonstrated. The work remains in the current phase.
RejectedThe proposed baseline is unacceptable or no longer aligned with Program objectives.
