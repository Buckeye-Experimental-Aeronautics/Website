BExA style guide

The single source of truth for how BExA looks: rules and live examples on one page, rendered with the site's real CSS. We iterate here; once signed off, the site is refactored to match. Items tagged Decision are proposals to accept, change or reject.

00Principles

  • Engineering, not marketing. Plain words, real numbers, no filler. Say it once.
  • The logo is the design language. Skew angle, taper line and scarlet come from the BExA mark; nothing decorative that isn't derived from it.
  • Homemade but professional. Real photos, honest labels, slightly technical. No stock icons or decorative gradients.
  • One size per role. If two things do the same job they look the same.
  • Dark by default, cream for emphasis. Red is an accent and a brand block, never body text.

01Color

Body text on dark is cream (primary) or gray (secondary), never pure white. Red text is for eyebrows, numbers and status only, at 18px+ or bold. Max one full-bleed red block per screen.

02Typography

Display Space Grotesk (700, −0.03em tracking) · Body Inter · Labels JetBrains Mono. Mobile sizes: H1 38, H2 30.
  • Headings are sentence case. A period only if the heading is a full sentence, imperative included (Become a sponsor.); a phrase or fragment gets none, single- or multi-word alike (Our teams, What we're up to).
  • No exclamation marks. Digits for numbers (30+, 15km); en-dash for ranges (25–100kg).
  • Button labels: verbs, 3 words or fewer; → only when linking to another page.

03Spacing & radius.

8px base unit. Page max width 1440px, side gutter 64px (24px at 900px and below). Section padding 72px vertical (48px mobile). Card grids: auto-fit,minmax(260px,1fr), 32px gap.
  • Two radii only. --r-s: 4px for small controls: buttons, status tags, inputs. --r-m: 8px for surfaces: cards, photos, tables, the team accordion, the program table.
  • Nothing over 8px. No pills, no circles.

04Buttons

05Arrow & taper line.

The arrow is redrawn from the one in the logo's X: a red blade that sweeps up and tapers to a point, ending in a dart head — swept-back upper barb, shorter lower barb, notched back. On the site the blade is the taper line and the dart is its head; the blade runs into the notch so they connect. Use as a divider sparingly; never on photos. The sticky bar uses it as scroll progress: bar --red-bar #8c0925, 80px condensing to 52px after 160px of scroll, tracer on the bar's bottom edge, nothing below it filled.

Points (tip, upper barb, notch, lower barb) relative to the tip: 0,0 · −36,−11 · −27.5,0 · −32.5,+6.8: traced from the logo, with the lower barb eased out slightly but still noticeably shorter than the upper, so the head stays lopsided like the mark. The blade runs into the notch, so blade and head read as one shape.

10Imagery, motion, responsive.

  • Imagery: real BExA photos only, object-fit:cover, 8px corners, no borders/shadows/overlays (except the accordion gradient). Hero is the night hangar jet. Sponsor logos are monochrome black on white; the sponsor strip is a 180px marquee, 22s, pauses on hover.
  • Motion: 0.3s ease for hover and condensing; 0.45s cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.28,1) for the accordion. DecisionAdd prefers-reduced-motion support.
  • Responsive: 900px (nav wraps, padding shrinks) and 640px (accordion stacks, mission table becomes a list, hero H1 38px). Desktop-first; nothing may cause horizontal scroll.
  • Links / focus: body links underline on hover only; nav links never underlined except the current page; focus ring 2px cream.
